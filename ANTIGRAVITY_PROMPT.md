## 🎯 THE MISSION (Why We're Doing This)

**Problem:** Automotive project managers lack real-time visibility into project health, budget execution, quality metrics, and risk forecasting.

**Solution:** Build an AI-powered analytics dashboard that delivers:
- Real-time project health scores (AI-driven)
- Automated risk detection & mitigation recommendations
- Quality predictions before defects occur
- Cost anomaly flagging for budget control

**Outcome:** 15-20% cost reduction + faster issue detection (1 hour vs. 1+ day) + proactive risk management
